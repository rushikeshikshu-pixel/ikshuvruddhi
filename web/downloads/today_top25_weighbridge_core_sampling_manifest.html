Standard Weighbridge Core-Sampling Protocol:
  1. Insert hydraulic core-sampler at 45° angle through the middle-third of the cane truck bed.
  2. Express fresh composite juice within 10 minutes of coring; record refractometric Brix (°Bx).
  3. Perform lead-acetate / dry-clarification filtration and record polarimetric Pol (sucrose %).
  4. Enforce fundamental law: Pol must be less than or equal to Brix. If Pol > Brix, re-clean instrument prisms and repeat test.
  5. Record Cut-to-Crush delay hours from farmer cutting slip timestamp.
