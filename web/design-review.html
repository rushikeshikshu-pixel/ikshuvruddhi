Selected: Field Operations, with an Executive Brief header

This best fits the daily users: agriculture managers need a quick seasonal read, while officers need Gat-level plots, area discrepancies, satellite freshness and a sample/harvest work queue. The main dashboard should therefore open with a compact KPI summary and put the searchable plot queue, map and follow-up actions directly below it.

  • Keep satellite acquisition dates and live/fallback status visible beside results.
  • Make area discrepancies and Gat-level checks easy to filter.
  • Keep harvest and laboratory sampling lists exportable for field teams.

The concepts are design previews; metric tiles are examples of layout, not a current operational report. The selected direction is the Field Operations theme, with its compact summary and plot workbench.
